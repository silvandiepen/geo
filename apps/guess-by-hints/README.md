# Guess By Hints

Guess a mystery country from progressive clues.

## How to Play
1. Up to 6 hints reveal progressively: continent, distance, direction, neighbors, population, capital initial
2. Each wrong guess reveals the next hint and costs points
3. Fewer guesses = higher score

## Scoring
- Correct on 1st guess: 100 pts
- Each wrong guess: -15 pts
- Minimum: 10 pts

## Development
